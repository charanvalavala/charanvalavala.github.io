03 / THE METHOD

01

Build a normal bank.

Select normal training images. Hold out a separate normal subset to calibrate the inspection threshold.

02

Compare local features.

A pretrained vision transformer turns the image into 256 patch vectors. Each patch finds its nearest normal match.

03

Inspect the difference.

Patch distances form the heatmap. The largest distances determine the image score. Review the source and reference together.
